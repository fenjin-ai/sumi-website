Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers LeftBlank for Mac and iPad and leftblank.app, published by Fenjin Wang.

## Your documents

LeftBlank edits and typesets documents locally on your device. We do not collect your manuscripts, exports or editing history, and do not operate a server that receives them. There is no advertising or analytics SDK in the app. You do not need a LeftBlank account.

## Network requests and optional services

When you browse the Typst package catalog, download packages, templates or sample books, LeftBlank contacts the relevant catalog or download host. Those providers receive the network information necessary to serve the request, such as your IP address. Opening a document that requires an unbundled package can trigger a package download; it does not upload your document to us. Third-party services have their own privacy policies.

If you use an iCloud-enabled build and turn on iCloud, document synchronization is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We cannot access your iCloud documents. The independently distributed Preview can check for updates and download releases.

## iPad subscriptions

Subscriptions on iPad are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store using
your Apple Account. LeftBlank checks verified subscription transactions on your
device to determine whether creating and editing are available. Apple handles
payment information, purchases, renewals, refunds and introductory-offer
eligibility under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card
details and do not operate a server that collects your subscription transactions
or links them to your documents. There is no LeftBlank account, advertising or
analytics SDK in the iPad app.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription
settings. When a subscription expires, existing documents remain available to
read and export, including their editable source and resources. You can delete
local documents on your device and manage iCloud data through Apple. Removing
the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

## This website

The website uses no analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. Language and appearance choices are stored only in your browser. The hosting provider processes network requests to deliver the site. Downloading examples does not require an account.

## Support and your choices

You can delete your local files and browser preferences yourself and manage iCloud data through Apple. If you choose to contact support or submit a public GitHub issue, the content you provide will be used to handle your request. Avoid including private manuscripts in public issues. For support or a privacy question, contact [support@leftblank.app](mailto:support@leftblank.app) or visit [LeftBlank support](https://github.com/leftblank-app/leftblank/issues).
